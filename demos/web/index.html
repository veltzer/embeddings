Word embeddings in the browser

Word vectors trained on Wikipedia by veltzer/embeddings, every language rotated into one shared space, so a word's neighbors can be looked up in another language. The tables here are the most frequent words of each language from release …, quantized to int8. Every lookup below is a dot product against every row, in plain JavaScript; a language's table (about 16 MB) loads the first time a form needs it.
